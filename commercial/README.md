# Commercial strategy — v0.0.8 candidate

[Issue #7](https://github.com/vixttha/Converium-HQ/issues/7). Owner review required. No sales or launch authorized. [Historical commercial proposal](../evidence/imported/2026-11-24/COMMERCIAL-OFFER.md) remains unchanged.

## Positioning

Six independent engineering capabilities with persistent, revision-bound knowledge. The HQ is an optional knowledge index, not a seventh product or unified install. Target first: solo developers and small teams already using coding agents who repeatedly lose context, struggle to prove bug fixes, or need reliable documentation. Secondary: maintainers who need traceable evidence without a proprietary execution platform.

Evidence-backed claims: 23 historical cases, 14 observed operational relationships, 64 fixture configurations and 192 native smoke checks, actual Graphify map. This supports practical usefulness and mechanical coexistence, not universal agent support, automatic security, guaranteed time savings, or any percentage reduction in bugs/tokens/cost. The 7/8 versus 7/8 pilot showed no measured advantage.

Research observed 2026-11-24; not an exhaustive market or feature comparison. Test the differentiation with actual prospective users.

## First paid offer hypotheses (USD, not published prices)

| Offer | Hypothesis | Delivery boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-guided adoption | Free where licensing permits | Independent docs and reviewed install pins; no paid runner or guaranteed support. |
| Guided setup | $99–149 one-time | One existing public repo, one or two chosen tools, scoped install review, basic validation and handoff; cap at 90 minutes. |
| Evidence readiness audit | $249–399 one-time | One repo, fixed list of checks, report and prioritized fixes; cap at four hours; no guarantee of security or release. |
| Training / support | $75–125 per hour | Owner-scheduled remote session; no unlimited SLA or mandatory subscription. |

Do not build a SaaS or shared backend to test demand. Do not charge for rights not granted. Price assumptions are hypotheses, not verified market prices. At two $149 setup sessions and one $299 audit in a month, hypothetical gross receipts are $597 before fees, taxes, support and time; not forecast revenue. For solo sustainability, measure actual time and decline work exceeding scope.

## Validation plan

Week 1: owner decides rights/name/offer boundaries and one product suitable for public adoption. Week 2: recruit five consenting developers through existing communities (no automated outreach); record tasks and blockers without private code disclosure. Week 3: run two bounded pilot onboarding tasks, measure effort and success against ordinary workflow, obtain opt-in testimonial approval. Week 4: evaluate demand (at least two serious paid inquiries or one paid scoped pilot, only after rights and offer approval), support load, and adoption friction. If none, refine the offer before expanding features. No revenue is claimed.

## Rights and launch gates

[Machine-readable license observations](rights-inventory.json) are pinned to source snapshots. Four MIT notices observed: [provenistem](https://github.com/vixttha/Provenistem/blob/d64d0c73538e79af0292642c1f23bafe2e979ec0/packages/provenistem/LICENSE), [lydite](https://github.com/vixttha/Lydite-Test-Vault-Template/blob/eae4c9d9d56ed132b400d7c0f56a9babfe5cba07/starter/test-vault/LICENSE), [veraphify](https://github.com/vixttha/Veraphify/blob/add4a029312c958a5e126990fc49e802c0112c27/LICENSE), [brand-identity](https://github.com/vixttha/Brand-Identity-Template/blob/74a4666404fb4aa427881d0907cacbc8c65e2cfc/brand-identity/LICENSE). No product-level grant observed for VIB and README HQ (README HQ contains historical BART notices only). Public visibility is not permission to redistribute. Check exact package scope, third-party assets, notices, license choices and owner authorization; HQ itself needs an explicit license/rights decision. Name conflict review in [brand](../brand/README.md) must precede public commercial branding. No software license or terms changed by this strategy.

Commercial readiness requires: owner-approved rights inventory and offer, mature independently installable product(s), reproducible setup evidence, private data handling/terms/refund policy, name clearance, and a reviewed public release plan. No public checkout, product bundling, subscription, billing, or marketing campaign activated.

## Claims and evaluation

Use the [historical evidence](../evidence/README.md) and [recovery criteria](../recovery/README.md) as proof, not as inflated marketing. Compare a fresh-agent ordinary workflow and tool-assisted workflow using prespecified equal tasks and score version, source fidelity, bugs, rework, time and tool calls; publish limitations. Never claim a gain from the old small pilot.
