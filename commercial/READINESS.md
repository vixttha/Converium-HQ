## Narrow first offer (proposed)

Guided Brand Identity setup, one public repository, one existing project, owner retains creative choices. Hypothesis: $99 introductory fixed scope, maximum 90 minutes. Deliver a reviewed kit install at the exact licensed release, a working manifest, written limitations, and a handoff. Do not promise a trademark search, artwork, completed logo, legal clearance, or audit certification. No charge until product/rights review and owner offer approval. If any defect affects this exact setup path, stop and offer correction or cancel; don't sell an unverified workflow.

Measure five voluntary discovery conversations, two scoped pilot setups, actual time, paid interest, defect reports, rework and consent for any testimonial. No outreach, payment account, purchase or marketing activation in this audit. Publish no guaranteed time/cost savings. Do not create another SaaS or bundle. If the owner prefers a different product, rerun the same readiness gates on its exact release.
